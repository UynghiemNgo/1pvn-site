Capabilities

Everything the programme needs, in one place

Four connected areas covering the full programme cycle — from public intake to final reporting.

Public Portal & Intake

Bring opportunities in

  • Guided opportunity submission wizard
  • Investor and partner registration
  • Document upload and consultation registration
  • Submission tracking and electronic signatures
Consultation Management

Run structured consultations

  • Agenda builder and participant registration
  • Live notes capture and attendance tracking
  • Minutes upload → AI extraction → structured summary
  • Action item tracking and policy-flag workflow
Investment Pipeline

Move projects forward

  • Staged pipeline with automated gate checks
  • Composite scoring and risk register per project
  • Investor matchmaking and B2B meeting tracking
  • MoU and partnership agreement workflow
Reporting & Documentation

Turn activity into outputs

  • KPI and indicator tracking
  • Complete, exportable audit trail
