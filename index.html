Data residency

Your network's data stays where you need it

Private network means private data. You pick the region, you get your own database, and the AI is wired to respect both.

Choose your region

Deployed where you say

1PVN runs in the region your organisation requires — EU USA Asia — application, database, and file storage together.

Your own database

Not pooled with anyone

Each network gets a dedicated database rather than shared rows in a multi-tenant table. Your records are separated at the infrastructure level, not by a filter in someone's query.

Residency-aware AI

AI that respects the border

An EU-resident model handles AI by default. Business cards are read on your own device — the image never reaches a vision API — and personal data is stripped before any fallback provider is used.

AI, with a human in the loop

AI handles the busywork — people make the decisions

Every AI output is a draft. Nothing is finalised or sent without a person reviewing it first.

Data entry

Notes into structured records

Meeting notes, business cards, and uploaded documents become clean CRM records — no manual re-typing.

Matching

Suggested matches, scored

Suggested pairings across your network with a score attached, so the strongest ones surface for review.

Drafting

First drafts in seconds

Briefs, summaries, and reports drafted from your own data — ready for you to refine and approve.
